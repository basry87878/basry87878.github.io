// About Stoisser

We exist to make convenience effortless

Stoisser was founded on the belief that vending machines should do more than just dispense snacks. They should be smart, reliable, and invisible — so you never have to think about them.

// Our mission

Put great food and drinks within arm's reach of every working person

Based in Graz, Austria, Stoisser brings modern vending solutions to offices, gyms, and public spaces across the region.

Our edge isn't the hardware. It's the system behind it: real-time inventory tracking, cashless payment, and a local team that responds within hours when something goes wrong.

We never charge for installation, and we handle every restock ourselves. Our clients earn revenue sharing. The machine pays for everyone.
